How to play

🌑 A construct floats in a beam of light, casting a shadow on the back wall.

👆 Drag to rotate it. On levels that need a twist, use two fingers (or the ⟲ ⟳ buttons / Q–E keys) to roll.

🎯 Line the shadow up with the faint target silhouette. The closer you get, the brighter it glows.

✨ Snap it in (90%+ match) to solve. Cleaner matches earn more stars.

🔦 Late levels add a second light — your object must cast two different shadows that match two targets at the same time.
